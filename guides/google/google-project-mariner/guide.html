05 • Prompt Engineering Writing Bulletproof Browser-Agent Mission Briefs

The 5-Part Project Mariner Mission Formula

Prompting a browser agent is different from prompting a chatbot. Ambiguous instructions cause agents to wander across 30 tabs; crisp boundaries turn Mariner into a laser-focused executive assistant.

Anatomy of a Gold-Standard Mariner Mission Prompt

1. Clear End Goal 2. Starting Sites / Portals 3. Hard Filters & Fallback Rules 4. Stop-Before Safety Boundary 5. Exact Output Schema
"Find the 4 best pet-friendly boutique hotels in Kyoto for Oct 14–18 (4 nights, 2 adults) starting on Google Hotels and verifying final room rates directly on each hotel's official booking page. Hard filters: total price including taxes & pet fees under $340/night, minimum 4.5 rating, within a 10-minute walk of a subway station; if a hotel hides pet fees, check its FAQ page. STOP before entering any guest details or clicking Reserve/Checkout. Return a Markdown table with Hotel Name, Nightly Total (incl. taxes/pet fee), Nearest Station Walk Time, Cancellation Policy, and Direct Booking URL."

4 Golden Rules for Reliable Browser Navigation

1 Pre-Answer "What If Out of Stock?"

Always include one fallback rule—such as "If unavailable on the target date, check +/- 1 day" or "Pick the closest organic size."

2 Cap Breadth With Explicit Numbers

Say "Inspect the top 5 non-sponsored listings" instead of "Check all listings" so Mariner finishes in 3 minutes instead of 25.

3 Demand All-In Final Pricing

Tell Mariner to click one step deeper to reveal mandatory cleaning fees, shipping, or taxes—where static search snippets lie!

4 Always Include a STOP BEFORE Clause

Even though Mariner has built-in safety gates, explicitly stating STOP at the review cart screen aligns its planner cleanly.

Teach & Repeat + Human-in-the-Loop Safety Gates

Every industry has quirky internal portals, multi-level dropdowns, or custom formatting habits that take 300 words to explain—but only 30 seconds to show. That is why Mariner introduced Teach and Repeat alongside strict confirmation gates.

LEFT • TEACH & REPEAT: Demonstrate a browser workflow once; Mariner generalizes it for new inputs.
RIGHT • CONFIRMATION GATE: Automatic hard pause before logins, payments, or irreversible clicks.

How to Record a Reusable "Teach & Repeat" Skill in 4 Steps

1 Click "Teach" & Perform Once

Start recording and click through your portal normally—opening specific filters, toggling tabs, and copying the exact metrics you care about.

2 Narrate the "Why" Out Loud

Speak or type quick hints as you go (e.g., "Always switch billing toggle to Annual first, and ignore Enterprise Call-Us tiers").

3 Review Learned Blueprint

Mariner converts your demonstration into a flexible, semantic workflow—not brittle pixel coordinates—turning dates or names into variables.

4 Replay With Fresh Inputs Anytime

Next Monday, just say: Run my Competitor Pricing Audit skill on Linear, Asana, and Monday.com—done in parallel!

09 • Trust & Security Security, Privacy, Session Hygiene & Smart Delegation Boundaries

Security, Privacy & Smart Delegation Habits

Giving an AI agent a live browser requires thoughtful guardrails. Google engineered Mariner around isolated virtual machines, prompt-injection defenses, and strict human confirmation—here is how to stay 100% safe.

How Mariner Protects Your Credentials, Cookies & Browsing

1 Isolated Cloud VMs

Public research tasks start in clean, sandboxed cloud browser containers—so random sites cannot read your local desktop files or personal Chrome tabs.

2 Zero-Chat Passwords

Never type passwords, SSNs, or credit card CVVs into a prompt! Always use Take Over to sign into the browser UI directly when needed.

3 Anti-Injection Shields

Mariner runs multi-layered classifiers to ignore malicious hidden instructions embedded on untrusted webpages attempting to hijack your agent's goal.

Traffic-Light Delegation Framework: Green, Yellow & Red Zones

Zone Task Examples Recommended Safety Posture
● GREEN ZONE
Full Parallel Autonomy		 Public price comparisons, flight/hotel discovery, vendor feature matrices, academic/event directory gathering, apartment filtering. Run up to 10 parallel unauthenticated Cloud VMs freely in the background; review exported table at the end.
● YELLOW ZONE
Supervised + Stop Gate		 Building logged-in grocery/retail carts, pre-filling complex government or travel forms, CRM lead research, restaurant booking prep. Sign in via Take Over; keep session scoped to that one site; inspect every cart item & field before clicking final Submit/Pay yourself.
● RED ZONE
Keep 100% Manual		 Wire transfers, stock/crypto trade execution, medical portal changes, signing binding legal contracts, password-manager administration. Do not delegate to browser agents. High-stakes financial, medical, and security settings belong strictly under direct human clicks.
30-Second Final Cart Audit Before You Click Pay
Even a 99%-accurate browser clicker can occasionally select a Family-Size 64oz jar instead of 16oz, or default to expedited shipping on a tricky retailer layout. Always glance at (1) Item quantities, (2) Delivery date & address, and (3) Final tax/shipping total during the confirmation handoff.

Troubleshooting Clinic & Pre-Flight Cheat Sheet

Live websites throw curveballs—CAPTCHA walls, infinite-scroll traps, and regional popups. Keep these 7 instant fixes handy whenever an agent session stalls.

Top 7 Mariner & Agent Mode Hiccups (And 10-Second Fixes)

Symptom / Browser Hiccup Root Cause & 10-Second Fix
Agent pauses on a "Verify You Are Human" / CAPTCHA screen By design! Google agents strictly refuse to bypass anti-bot challenges autonomously. Click Take Over, complete the check yourself in 3 seconds (or switch to an official airline/retailer site with cleaner access), and click Resume.
Agent keeps scrolling an endless feed without stopping Infinite-scroll page + unbounded prompt. Click Pause and add a hard ceiling: "Inspect only the first 8 non-sponsored results sorted by Highest Rated, then stop and summarize."
Reported hotel or ticket price is lower than checkout reality Site hid resort/service fees on page 1. Instruct Mariner: "Click through to the pre-checkout price breakdown screen (without booking) to capture all mandatory taxes and fees."
Modal popup or newsletter overlay blocks clicks Stubborn sticky overlay. Usually Mariner clicks the X automatically; if a custom popup traps focus, tap Take Over, press Esc once, and resume.
"Teach & Repeat" skill breaks after a portal redesign Target site removed an entire workflow step. Open the saved skill, edit the plain-English step instructions (or re-record just the changed 15-second segment), and save.
Cart items picked wrong package size or store location Store zip code defaulted to cloud VM region. Always specify your delivery zip code in the prompt—or sign in via Take Over first so your saved address loads.
Task times out on a massive 20-site research prompt Single browser overloaded with sequential work. Split the list into 5 parallel tasks of 4 sites each in the Cloud Fleet sidebar for 4x faster completion!

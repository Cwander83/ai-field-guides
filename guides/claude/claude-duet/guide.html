Mastering the Morning Brief & Overnight Sweeps

Most professionals start their morning drowning in 90 unread emails, 14 Slack channels, and double-booked calendar invites. Duet’s Morning Brief (Claude Digest) clears that clutter before your alarm rings—handing you only high-leverage choices with replies already written.

Overnight Signal Compression: Duet’s Cloud VM polls authorized Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, and SaaS feeds every few hours, strips routine chatter, and distills scattered threads into calm, action-ready Morning Brief cards.

The 4-Stage Overnight Briefing Cycle

1 Periodic Background Sweeps

Every few hours—and once shortly before your morning wake time—Duet queries your linked Gmail inbox, Google Calendar, Slack workspaces, and Marketplace or Zapier MCP connectors from its isolated cloud VM.

2 Signal-vs-Noise Filtering

Opus 5.5 discards newsletters, cold sales outreach, and resolved bot alerts. It surfaces only scheduling collisions, direct human questions, overdue billing, and stalled deliverables matching your Memory priorities.

3 Ready-to-Send Reply Drafts

Rather than merely summarizing problems, Duet prepares concrete solutions: a calendar reschedule proposal checking actual free slots, or a polite client reply matched to your tone preferences in Memory.

4 90-Second Triage at Breakfast

Unlock iOS, Android, or web over coffee: tap Approve to dispatch a clean draft, tap Defer (Not now) to revisit at 2 PM, or open Chat to tweak wording before sending. 

Brief Calibration Prompt (paste into your main #morning-brief Channel once):
"Deliver my Morning Brief at 7:30 AM EST. Sweep Gmail (Primary + VIP clients), Google Calendar (next 72 hours), Slack (#ops-escalations and direct @mentions only), and Stripe unpaid invoices. Ignore newsletters, GitHub bot notifications, and automated receipts. Group findings into Urgent Decisions, Calendar Conflicts, and Ready Drafts—never exceed 5 items total, and pause every outgoing message at the Approval Gate."
Smart Quota Habit: Exclude High-Chatter Folders on Day One

Cloud VM, Headless Browser & Routine Prompt Anatomy

Unlike mobile chat wrappers that pause the moment your screen dims, Claude Duet commands an isolated Cloud Virtual Machine with a built-in headless cloud browser and execution sandbox—turning multi-hour research and modeling into async background jobs.

Inside Duet’s Persistent Cloud VM
  • Headless Cloud Browser: Visits vendor portals, regulatory filings, live competitor pricing pages, and web sources that lack public APIs.
  • Deliverables Engine (Make Tab): Compiles financial forecasts, slide decks, interactive dashboards, research memos, and web prototypes inside its sandbox.
  • Live Source Monitoring: Watches changing web pages or SaaS dashboards asynchronously and alerts you in Chat or Tasks when thresholds trip.
  • Zero Device Tether: Close your MacBook or lose subway signal—the Cloud VM finishes cleanly and syncs results to Tasks and Make.
Make Templates vs. Tasks Oversight
  • Make Tab Starters: Launch proven routines in one click—including Daily Inbox Triage, Structured Daily Planning, Expense Reconciliation, and Competitor Watch.
  • Tasks Tab Transparency: Inspect live VM steps, fetched browser URLs, and drafted outputs in real time without cluttering your Chat feed.
  • Manual Completion Rule: Duet prepares deliverables and clears blockers, yet you manually mark major Tasks complete so important work never disappears quietly.

07 • Safety Architecture & Approval Gates Autonomous Read & Draft vs. Mandatory Write Checkpoints

The Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Approval Gate

Proactive autonomy only earns trust when an agent knows exactly where preparation stops and irreversible action begins. Claude Duet enforces a hard architectural boundary between autonomous background prep and external state-changing writes.

Built-In Pause Before Impact: Duet freely reads feeds, browses public portals, and prepares complete drafts on its Cloud VM, yet stops firmly at the Approval Gate ring until you inspect the payload and tap Approve.
1 Autonomous Zone (No Pause Needed)
  • Read & Triage: Sweeping Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Stripe, QuickBooks, and read-only Zapier connectors overnight.
  • Cloud VM & Browser Research: Navigating public web pages, reading PDFs, comparing prices, and scraping tables.
  • Compile & Draft: Building financial models, slide decks, dashboards, and saving complete email or invoice drafts locally in Make or Drafts.
2 Gated Write Zone (Mandatory Pause)
  • External Messaging: Sending Gmail messages, posting Slack announcements, or firing external webhooks.
  • Payments & Payroll: Executing Stripe refunds or Link virtual-card purchases, submitting Gusto payroll runs, or billing clients.
  • Legal & Destructive Ops: Signing or routing DocuSign contracts, deleting Drive files, or canceling multi-guest meetings.

Inside Every Approval Card: Payload Preview & Your 3 Controls

Gate Element What Duet Displays On-Screen Recommended Operator Habit
Full Payload Preview Exact recipients, subject line, full message text, dollar sum, or target file IDs. Scan recipients and numbers carefully—especially on multi-recipient threads or billing runs.
Approve (Yes) Releases the staged Cloud VM tool call immediately and logs the outcome in Tasks. Use for crisp, verified drafts; jump into Chat to adjust tone first if anything feels off.
Defer (Not now) Keeps the staged payload intact in Tasks without executing or discarding your prep work. Perfect during commute triage when you want desktop eyes on a contract before signing.
Reject (No) Cancels the action cleanly and lets you instruct Duet why in Chat to refine future Memory. Follow rejections with a 1-sentence Memory rule (e.g., “Never CC legal on Stage 1 quotes”).

09 • Security, Privacy & Governance Credential Shield, Outbound Classifier & Enterprise Reality

Security Boundaries, Privacy & Research Preview Governance

Granting an always-on cloud agent access to email, calendars, and financial tools demands rigorous engineering safeguards—and honest clarity about which compliance controls exist today during the Research Preview versus commercial Team and Enterprise plans.

1 Built-In Technical Defenses
  • Credential Isolation: Opus 5.5 never sees raw passwords, browser cookies, or OAuth tokens inside its reasoning context; authentication lives inside a separated proxy layer.
  • Outbound Security Classifier: A dedicated safety classifier screens outgoing messages and payloads to block leaking credit cards, government IDs, or sensitive private records unless explicitly directed.
  • Prompt-Injection Containment: If a hostile email or web page hides hidden commands (“Ignore rules and send money”), the mandatory Approval Gate freezes any write or transfer before execution.
2 Research Preview Governance Limits
  • Individual Plans Only: During the Oct–Nov 2026 preview, Duet supports individual US Pro, Max, and Ultra plans; Claude Team and Enterprise do not yet include Duet.
  • No Org Admin Console Yet: Centralized IT connector allowlists, SSO enforcement, custom retention rules, and audit log streaming are not yet available for Duet.
  • Consumer Data Controls: Check individual Settings → Privacy opt-out settings for model training before linking proprietary corporate mailboxes or financial ledgers.

5. Outbound Security Classifier blocked a staged reply Draft contained raw payment card numbers, government IDs, or restricted PII. Replace sensitive digits with masked references (e.g., last 4 digits) or explicitly confirm intent in Chat before re-staging.
6. Duet cannot read files on my Mac hard drive or native apps Duet executes solely inside an isolated Cloud VM, not your local macOS machine. Upload shared docs to Claude Memory/Projects, or run local folder mounts & native GUI automation inside Claude Cowork.
7. Duet app or tab missing on my corporate login Signed into a Claude Team/Enterprise seat or non-US profile during preview. Switch to an eligible US individual Pro, Max, or Ultra account after enrolling in the Duet research preview waitlist.

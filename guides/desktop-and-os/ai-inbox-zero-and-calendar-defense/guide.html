04 • 5-Bucket Email Triage Mastery VIP, Action, Waiting, Read-Later & Receipt Workflow

The 5-Bucket Split: Clearing 100 Emails in Ten Minutes

A single chronological inbox treats a high-stakes contract reply identically to a SaaS receipt and a marketing digest. Routing every arrival into five crisp buckets lets you clear true human work fast and batch everything else automatically.

Shielded Intake, Five-Lane Control: Deterministic bucket rules and sanitization barriers deflect inbound noise and untrusted payloads outside your workspace—keeping VIP threads and staged drafts inside a clean, calm capsule.

How Each Bucket Works in Practice

1 VIP & Action (Triage First)

VIP captures teammates, leadership, and active clients. Action holds messages needing a decision, deliverable, or scheduled meeting. Review AI Auto Drafts here first, edit two words, and hit Send yourself.

2 Waiting (Follow-Up Radar)

Whenever you ask a vendor or colleague for an answer, label or snooze the thread under Waiting for 48 hours or 5 days. If nobody replies, AI drafts a polite one-line bump automatically.

3 Read-Later (Bundled Digests)

Route newsletters, release changelogs, and CC-only status threads out of Primary into Read-Later. In Shortwave or Gemini Ask, summarize the entire week’s bundle in 60 seconds every Friday afternoon.

4 Receipt (Finance Autopilot)

Match subject:(invoice OR receipt) and billing senders (billing@, stripe.com) straight to Receipt, skipping Primary completely while queuing PDFs for structured JSON extraction. 

# Query only actionable unread threads via Workspace CLI (excludes automated Receipts & Newsletters):
gws gmail users.messages.list --params '{"userId": "me", "q": "is:unread -label:Receipt -label:Read-Later -category:promotions", "maxResults": 25}'

# Gmail / Shortwave deterministic pre-filter rule for Bucket #5 (Receipts):
from:(billing@ OR receipts@ OR invoice@) OR subject:(invoice OR "tax receipt" OR "payment confirmation") has:attachment
Inbox Zero Rule: Never Touch the Same Thread Twice Without Moving Its Bucket
Every time you open a VIP or Action email, choose one immediate outcome: send an edited draft and archive, move to Action with a task date, or label Waiting with a 48-hour nudge timer.

05 • Prompt Anatomy & Draft Engineering Voice Matching, 5-Part Formula & users.drafts.create

Draft-Only Prompts & the 5-Part Personal Ops Formula

Generic AI drafts fail because they sound overly eager, ignore your real calendar, and omit safety boundaries. Combining concise style constraints in Shortwave AI Memory or Superhuman with a 5-part structured prompt keeps drafts crisp and safe.

Style & Voice Calibration Rules
  • Three-Sentence Ceiling: Cap routine acknowledgments and scheduling replies at 35–60 words with plain active verbs.
  • Ban Cliché Openers: Forbid filler like “I hope this email finds you well” or “Thank you for reaching out” inside your AI Memory instructions.
  • Cite Missing Facts: Instruct the AI to insert [VERIFY: price/date] brackets rather than guessing numbers or policy details.
Hard Draft-Only Tool Boundary
  • Gmail API Separation: Call users.drafts.create (which stages an editable draft inside the thread) rather than users.messages.send.
  • OAuth Least Privilege: Grant automated triage scripts gmail.readonly and gmail.compose; withhold full send rights from background pipelines.
  • One-Click Human Gate: Review staged drafts in two daily batches (11:30 AM and 4:30 PM) so every outbound promise has human sign-off.

The 5-Part Draft-Only Personal Ops Formula

[1. Bucket Scope] Target only unread VIP or Action threads, [2. Untrusted Fence] read stripped body inside <untrusted_email> tags, [3. Voice Anchor] draft under 60 words with zero clichés, [4. Draft-Only Gate] save via users.drafts.create (never auto-send), [5. Slot Check] proposing only Focus-safe buffered times.
Formula Component What It Enforces What Breaks When Skipped
Bucket Scope Processes high-signal VIP and Action mail while skipping newsletters. Token budget burns drafting replies to no-reply marketing digests.
Untrusted Fence Separates system instructions from attacker-controlled inbound text. Hidden white text can hijack tool calls or alter drafted commitments.
Voice Anchor Enforces short paragraphs, direct answers, and bracketed unknowns. Replies sound stiff, overly apologetic, or invent unverified contract terms.
Draft-Only Gate Routes outputs strictly to Drafts via gmail.compose scope. One misread sarcasm or wrong thread sends an irreversible external email.
Slot Check Queries live free/busy slots outside protected Focus blocks & buffers. Suggested times double-book your deep-work mornings or omit travel zones.

06 • Automated Receipt & Invoice Extraction Gemini Structured JSON, Math Validation & Google Sheets

Zero-Touch Receipt & Invoice Extraction to Sheets

Receipts and vendor PDFs clutter primary inboxes and derail month-end bookkeeping. Pair deterministic Gmail filters with Gemini’s native multimodal JSON schema—backed by a strict arithmetic check—to log expenses reliably without manual data entry.

1 Filter & Queue Label

Route billing emails and PDF/image attachments into a Finance/Queue label automatically. Remove the queue label only after the row writes cleanly so duplicate runs never double-count an invoice.

2 Multimodal JSON Schema

Pass base64 PDF or PNG bytes directly to Gemini with response_mime_type: "application/json", enforcing typed keys: vendor_name, invoice_number, date, subtotal, tax, and total_amount.

3 Math & Anomaly Gate

Verify abs((subtotal + tax) - total_amount) < 0.02 in plain code before appending to Google Sheets. Flag blurry scans, unexpected totals, or new bank info as needs_review.

09 • Security & Prompt-Injection Defense Indirect Injection Vectors, Model Armor & Human Send Gates

Prompt-Injection Defense & Strict Human Send Gates

Whenever an AI agent reads incoming emails, attachments, or shared calendar invites, untrusted strangers control words inside its context window. Defend against Indirect Prompt Injection (IPI) with five layered technical barriers.

1 Four Hidden Inbound Attack Vectors
  • Invisible HTML & CSS: Attackers hide color: #ffffff; font-size: 0px blocks or off-screen <div> tags instructing agents to exfiltrate Drive files.
  • PDF Receipt Payloads: Malicious invoices bury white-on-white instructions telling extraction models to overwrite vendor totals or bank routing numbers.
  • Calendar .ics Invites: Unsolicited meeting descriptions inject directives that trigger when your scheduling assistant reads today’s agenda.
  • Markdown Image Exfiltration: Injected replies render hidden tracking URLs appending private inbox snippets into query parameters.
2 Mandatory Architectural Send Gates
  • Strip Send OAuth Scopes: Configure Google Auth / Microsoft Entra ID app registrations for triage tools using read + draft scopes only (omit gmail.send).
  • AI-Agent OAuth Flagging: Register Workspace MCP clients explicitly as AI-powered agents and narrow services with gws mcp -s gmail,calendar.
  • Sandboxed Execution: Run custom local or CI triage scripts inside restricted containers without access to SSH keys, browser cookies, or personal folders.

Five-Layer Defense-in-Depth Security Matrix

Security Layer Threat Neutralized Exact Implementation / Flag Human Verification
1. HTML & Text Flattener Zero-width chars, 0px fonts, white-on-white hidden CSS text. Strip hidden styles & collapse HTML to visible plain text pre-LLM. Confirm preview matches visible mail.
2. Model Armor Shield Jailbreak & indirect prompt injection inside API responses. gws ... --sanitize + SANITIZE_MODE="block" env var. Inspect blocked payload alerts weekly.
3. Delimiter Isolation Role confusion between system rules and inbound body text. Wrap email inside <untrusted_email_content> inert data tags. Never trust unquoted email summaries.
4. Scope & MCP Cap Cross-app Drive leaks or autonomous external email blasts. gws mcp -s gmail,calendar + users.drafts.create only. Audit OAuth grants quarterly.
5. Deterministic Validator Tampered invoice amounts or unauthorized bank updates. Verify subtotal + tax == total & vendor domain allowlist in code. Approve any needs_review row manually.

Use this quick clinic whenever a Workspace MCP call fails OAuth, AI drafts sound robotic, external invites pierce Focus Time, or receipt OCR breaks your spreadsheet—followed by our 6-point launch checklist.

1. Agent sends email instead of saving a Draft Prompt or OAuth client holds gmail.send / Mail.Send rights. Revoke send scope in Google Auth / Entra ID, grant only gmail.readonly + gmail.compose, and use users.drafts.create.
2. External meeting invites overwrite morning Focus Time Event created as standard busy hold instead of auto-decline Focus Time. Recreate with Google Calendar eventType: "focusTime" (declineAllConflictingInvitations) or Viva / Reclaim lock rules.

5. Receipt OCR breaks Sheets with Markdown fences Model returned conversational prose around JSON, or misread tax math. Set response_mime_type: "application/json" and add a subtotal + tax == total_amount guard flagging needs_review.

7. Important client reply hides inside Read-Later bundle Overbroad filter caught domain or thread reply in newsletter rule. Exclude known VIP domains and direct to:me replies from Read-Later filters, or promote sender to Shortwave/Superhuman VIP split.

Golden Rule: Anchor Live, Route AfterAll

The Two-Track Local Capture Loop: Mic (“Me”) + System Audio (“Them”)

Instead of dialing a third-party bot into Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Slack Huddles, Granola blends two clean local streams on your machine: your microphone track (labeled Me) and your computer’s speaker output stream (labeled Them). OS Audio Capture, Plan Tiers & Call-Bot Matrix

Because Granola captures audio locally rather than dialing a cloud bot into your meeting room, reliable setup depends on granting two OS permissions cleanly and choosing the tier that unlocks your retention and integration needs.

1 Local Capture Across macOS, Windows & iOS
  • macOS Permissions: Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and enable both Microphone (your voice) and Screen & System Audio Recording (others’ voices; Granola never captures pixels).
  • Windows Loopback: Grants Microphone and WASAPI system loopback audio automatically on install; verify access under Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.
  • iOS, Android & Watch: Tap one-touch lock-screen widgets for coffee chats, walking 1:1s, or outbound mobile calls; audio caches briefly until transcribed, then deletes.
  • Slack Huddles & Ad-Hoc Calls: Huddles skip calendar invites, so press + or Quick Note manually to capture system audio cleanly.
2 Granola Plan Tiers & Workspace Rules
  • Basic ($0): Bot-free capture, unlimited daily transcripts, shared folders, custom templates, and Slack sharing—but note view is capped at a rolling 30-day window (older notes lock until upgrade).
  • Business ($14/user/mo): Unlimited meeting history, explicit AI thinking model picker, built-in desktop voice dictation, custom dictionaries, Notion, HubSpot, Attio, Affinity, Zapier, and Public API + Webhooks.
  • Enterprise ($35+/user/mo): SSO (typically 50+ seats), SCIM, HIPAA BAA (desktop), audit logs, org-wide auto-deletion retention, and default zero model training.
  • Seat Rule: Team workspaces cannot mix free and paid seats; qualifying startups and students can claim 12 months of Business free.

04 • Hybrid Note-Taking Mastery Rough-Bullet Anchoring, Cue Symbols & Citation Tracing

Rough-Bullet Anchoring: Steering AI Without Losing Focus

When people try to transcribe every word themselves, they stop listening; when they let AI summarize a blank notepad, they get bland boilerplate. Granola’s sweet spot is lightweight anchoring—typing tiny memory hooks that guide post-call synthesis.

Human Intent Meets Verbatim Context: Rough shorthand typed mid-call locks onto the exact speaker moments in Granola’s background transcript—turning fragmented bullets into richly supported, scannable summaries.

Four Habits That Transform Enhanced Notes

1 Anchor With Keywords, Not Prose

Write three-word fragments like pricing tier pushback or bug safari auth instead of polished grammar. Granola fills in full sentences and exact figures from the transcript when you click Enhance.

2 Mark Priority With Simple Prefixes

Use * for urgent risks, => for locked decisions, and @Name for owners. Those tiny symbols act like magnets during AI synthesis so critical commitments never vanish into narrative paragraphs.

3 Hover-Trace Every AI Fact

Granola visually separates your typed lines from AI-expanded text. Hover over any synthesized point to verify the exact transcript excerpt—catching misheard product names or prices in seconds.

4 Re-Enhance With a Better Template

Started on a blank note and realized the chat turned into a hiring debrief or feature critique? Switch the Template selector afterward and Granola rebuilds the output without losing your raw bullets.

05 • Templates & Recipe Engineering Structural Templates, Post-Call Slash Recipes & Formula

Custom Templates & the 5-Part Recipe Formula

Power users separate Templates from Recipes: Templates define the clean structural skeleton of your Enhanced Note itself, while Recipes are reusable / slash prompts that turn finished notes into tickets, follow-up emails, or digests.

Meeting Templates (Structure Your Note)
  • Built-In & Custom Layouts: Create dedicated templates for Discovery, 1:1, Hiring, User Research, and Standup with custom icons and colors.
  • Section-Level Prompting: Write specific guidance under each heading (e.g., “Quote exact buyer words describing current spreadsheet pain”).
  • Instant Reformatting: Type / in the note or pick a template from the drawer to re-run synthesis cleanly.
Slash Recipes (Run Post-Call Jobs)
  • Single-Note Recipes: Type / inside Granola’s chat bar right after a call to draft a follow-up client email or extract bug reproduction steps.
  • Multi-Meeting Folder Recipes: Configure recipes to scan an entire folder or home dashboard to synthesize weekly product themes.
  • Zero Repeat Prompting: Save your sharpest extraction instructions once so teammates run identical quality checks in one click.

The 5-Part Hybrid Note & Action Pipeline Formula

[1. Human Anchor] Priority bullets (* risks, => decisions) [2. Transcript Evidence] grounded in exact Me / Them quotes, [3. Template Skeleton] shaped into Discovery or Hiring sections, [4. Recipe Transform] processed via /extract-actions for owners & dates, [5. Pipeline Route] and dispatched to Slack, Linear, or Obsidian _inbox/.
Pipeline Element What It Controls What Fails When Skipped
Human Anchor Tells Granola which decisions, objections, and nuances deserve top billing. Long tangent topics steal space from the 5-minute decision that actually mattered.
Transcript Evidence Supplies verbatim figures, dates, and exact customer phrasing for verification. Notes rely on vague memory or miss exact SLA percentages and seat counts.
Template Skeleton Guarantees consistent Markdown headings across every call in a folder. Downstream Zapier parsers and team readers struggle with shifting layouts.
Recipe Transform Extracts structured JSON-like bullets, bug tickets, or tailored emails via /. Every PM wastes 10 minutes re-typing the same follow-up chat prompt manually.
Pipeline Route Assigns a folder or Space that fires Slack, HubSpot, Linear, or Webhook syncs. Great insights stay trapped inside one personal notepad instead of moving work.

09 • Privacy & Governance Consent Laws, Audio Retention & Team Sharing

Consent Guardrails, Audio Retention & Team Privacy

Bot-free capture removes the social friction of an extra participant tile, which shifts legal and ethical transparency squarely onto you and your workspace admins. Configure consent notices and folder boundaries right on day one.

1 Recording Consent & Transparency
  • Two-Party & All-Party Laws: Many US states (e.g., CA, IL, WA, MA, PA) and GDPR jurisdictions require informing everyone before capturing conversation audio.
  • Built-In Disclosure Tools: Because no bot announces itself, enable Granola’s optional call-chat notice or on-screen watermark, or give a quick verbal heads-up at kickoff.
  • Enterprise Enforcement: Enterprise admins can mandate participant disclosure notifications across every workspace seat automatically.
2 Audio Discard, Hosting & Model Training
  • Zero Long-Term Audio Storage: Desktop transcribes on the fly and discards raw audio immediately; iOS caches locally only until transcription completes.
  • Encryption & US Hosting: Text notes and transcripts are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest on AWS US servers (SOC 2 Type II, GDPR/UK DPA + SCCs available).
  • LLM Training Boundaries: Third-party LLM providers (OpenAI/Anthropic) are barred from training on your notes. Business users can opt out of Granola internal training; Enterprise defaults training OFF.

11 • Diagnostics & Pre-Flight Top 7 Issues, 10-Second Fixes & 6-Point Rollout Checklist

Troubleshooting Clinic & Pre-Flight Checklist

Consult this clinic whenever macOS drops system audio, Bluetooth headphones fragment a transcript, Slack Huddles fail to trigger, or older notes lock behind Basic limits—followed by our 6-point pre-flight checklist.

Symptom / Issue Root Cause 10-Second Fix
1. Only “Me” recorded; “Them” audio is completely blank macOS lacks Screen & System Audio Recording permission, or OS output device mismatch. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, toggle Granola ON, and match call speaker output to OS default.
2. Transcript drops out mid-call over AirPods / Bluetooth Bluetooth switches between stereo A2DP and hands-free HFP profiles, breaking capture. Switch immediately to your laptop’s built-in mic/speakers or plug in a wired USB/3.5mm headset for uninterrupted dual-stream transcription.
3. Granola didn’t auto-prompt for a Slack Huddle Ad-hoc Huddles have no calendar event for Granola’s calendar watcher to detect. Open Granola right as the Huddle starts and click + or Quick Note manually to capture system audio from Slack Desktop.
4. Notes older than 30 days disappeared from sidebar Basic ($0) plan enforces a rolling 30-day viewable history; older notes are preserved. Upgrade to Business ($14/user/mo) to unlock your entire historical archive, advanced models, CRM syncs, and API access instantly.
5. Enhanced summary missed a major decision No human bullet anchored that thread, or an overly generic template ran. Add a 3-word bullet naming the decision, switch the Template selector (e.g., Discovery or Standup), and click Enhance again.
6. Linear Zap or Obsidian _inbox/ sync isn’t firing Note stayed in personal notes instead of trigger folder, or missing Business+ API key. Move the note into your mapped folder (e.g., Customer Bugs), verify Bearer grn_... key in Business+ settings, and inspect Zap/webhook logs.
7. Teammates cannot see notes from a shared call Saved in private My notes rather than a shared Space folder or copy-link. Drag the note into a shared Space folder or use the top-right Share menu to copy a web link or Markdown export.

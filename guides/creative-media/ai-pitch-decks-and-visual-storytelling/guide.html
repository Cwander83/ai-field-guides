04 • Doc-to-Deck Structuring Card Splits, Text Modes & Outline Anchoring

Doc-to-Deck Structuring: Turning Raw Notes Into Clean Cards

The biggest mistake new users make is dumping a four-page memo into a generic AI prompt box and hoping for magic. Controlling card boundaries and choosing the right text-interpretation mode eliminates overcrowded slides on the first generation.

Order Before Ornament: Explicit --- separators and deliberate text-mode settings turn messy notes into balanced single-claim cards—preventing merged topics and tiny unreadable fonts.

Choose the Right Text Mode Before Clicking Generate

1 Condense

Best for long Google Docs, PRDs, or board memos. Distills verbose prose into crisp card headlines and 3-bullet takeaways without inventing outside claims.

2 Generate

Best when starting from a 3-sentence prompt or rough skeleton. Expands short topic headings into supporting copy, comparison columns, and visual cues.

3 Preserve

Mandatory for audited financials, legal terms, customer quotes, or approved messaging. Keeps your exact wording verbatim while upgrading card layout. 

# Paste into Gamma ("Paste in text" -> Freeform card breaks) using --- dividers:
Title: Self-Serve Onboarding Investment — Executive Decision Deck
---
Recommendation: Fund 2 Engineers & $40k Tooling to Unlock $2.4M Expansion ARR
- Answer up front: 68% of Q3 trial drop-offs happen before first export
- Payback window: 4.5 months once activation rate lifts from 32% to 48%
---
Evidence: Q3 Activation Funnel (14,200 Trial Signups)
- Signup: 100% | Workspace Setup: 61% | First Export: 32% | Paid Team: 9.4%
The --- Divider Rule: One Persuasive Claim Per Card
Typing three hyphens (---), three asterisks (***), or invoking /split tells Gamma exactly where one card ends and the next begins. Never let more than one major chart or decision sit inside a single card boundary.

05 • Brand Lock & Prompt Formula Theme Builder Controls & 5-Part Storytelling Anatomy

Theme Brand Lock & The 5-Part Narrative Prompt Formula

Locking your visual identity inside Theme Builder and pairing it with a five-part narrative prompt stops AI decks from looking like generic templates—ensuring investor credibility from the very first draft.

1 Theme Builder & File Import
  • Import Existing Masters: Open Customize → Themes → + New Theme and click Import a file on an existing .pptx or Google Slides master to extract palette, fonts, and logo automatically.
  • Custom Typography: Upload brand .ttf or .otf fonts for Headings and Body, tuning line height and letter spacing cleanly.
  • Workspace Share & Lock: Copy a Theme share link for teammates, or enable Enterprise theme locks so staff can only pick approved brand kits.
2 Image Source & Visual Guardrails
  • Cohesive Visual Direction: Lock one image style token (e.g., minimalist architectural 3D render, dark violet studio lighting) instead of mixing cartoons and stock photos.
  • Zero-Text Image Rule: AI image generators frequently misspell small labels; instruct visuals to contain no text and keep all numbers in editable HTML/SVG blocks.
  • WCAG Surface Check: Ensure body text maintains crisp contrast on tinted card surfaces before exporting to boardroom projectors.

PowerPoint Export, Google Slides & Gemini Co-Pilot

When enterprise procurement teams, board directors, or Workspace collaborators need native slide decks instead of web links, a structured export-and-polish pipeline preserves editable shapes, tables, and speaker notes without manual rebuilds.

Stage-Ready Polish Across Formats: Bringing structured cards, data tokens, and speaker notes onto one clean stage ensures your deck lands smoothly whether delivered live or exported to .pptx and Google Slides.
1 Gamma → .pptx → Google Slides
  • Export Editable Objects: Click Share → Export → PowerPoint (.pptx) or export directly to Google Slides. Gamma preserves text boxes, tables, and shapes cleanly.
  • Use Zoom to Fit: Toggle Zoom to fit in export settings and split overloaded multi-column cards first so tall cards never clip off bottom slide edges.
  • Reverse Import Works Too: Upload an old, messy Google Slides or .pptx link back into Gamma to re-theme and modernize legacy decks in seconds.
2 Gemini in Google Slides (Native Flow)
  • Start From Drive Sources: Open slides.new, click the Ask Gemini sparkle panel, and click Add from Drive to ground slides in Docs, Sheets, or PDFs.
  • Match Presentation Style: Attach an approved corporate deck via Match presentation style so Gemini inherits your company theme automatically.
  • Approve Outline First: Gemini proposes an editable outline and asks clarifying questions before generating native Google Slides and speaker notes.

11 • Diagnostics & Launch Readiness Top 7 Issues, 10-Second Fixes & 6-Point Pre-Flight Checklist

Troubleshooting Clinic & Pre-Flight Checklist

Consult this diagnostic table whenever an exported PowerPoint shifts alignment, credits burn too quickly, custom fonts fall back, or analytics go blank—then run the 6-point pre-flight check before sending any high-stakes deck.

Symptom / Issue Root Cause 10-Second Fix
1. Exported .pptx or Google Slides clips or overlaps Fluid vertical card exceeded 16:9 height or packed dense nested columns. Enable Zoom to fit on export, split tall cards via /split, and simplify multi-column layouts before exporting.
2. AI merged three separate topics onto one crowded card Raw notes were pasted without explicit card break separators. Add --- or *** between sections before generation and choose Condense or Preserve instead of open-ended Generate.
3. Custom brand fonts reverted to Arial on a client’s laptop Recipient machine or Google Slides lacks the custom local .ttf/.otf file. Pair Google Fonts (like Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans) in Theme Builder for editable .pptx handoffs, or send PDF/web links.
4. AI credits ran out after generating just two decks High-cost AI image models ran across every single card automatically. Draft outline first with none or stock visuals, then generate custom AI artwork strictly on 3–4 key hero cards.
5. Can’t highlight or edit single words in Studio Mode Studio Mode fuses copy and illustration into a single raster image frame. Convert KPI, pricing, and legal slides to standard block-based Fluid Cards while keeping Studio Mode only for visual covers.
6. Per-card viewer analytics show zero card breakdown Deck was published as a Gamma Site or shared from a Free/Plus account. Use Pro+ plans for granular card telemetry and share as a standard tracked presentation link rather than a site.
7. Large 40-card deck stalls or errors during export A live web embed, oversized media block, or total length timed out render. Test a PDF export to pinpoint the blocking card, replace live embeds with static images, or export in two smaller batches.

✓
1. Single-Claim Card Headlines: Every card title states an active takeaway with metrics up front—not a vague label like “Overview.”
✓

✓
6. Export Integrity Verification: Opened exported .pptx, PDF, or Google Slides deck locally to confirm zero clipped charts or broken embeds.
